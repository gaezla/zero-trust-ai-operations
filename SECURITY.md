# Security policy

This repository contains a specification and a checklist, not software, so there is nothing here to patch. If you find an error in a rule or a checklist question, open an [issue](../../issues).

## Reporting a vulnerability in GAEZLA

To report a vulnerability in GAEZLA itself, follow the process at [gaezla.com/legal/security](https://gaezla.com/legal/security) and email **<info@gaezla.com>**. Please do not open a public issue for it.

GAEZLA commits to:

- acknowledge receipt within 2 business days;
- triage and respond with a remediation plan or rejection within 10 business days;
- not pursue legal action against good-faith researchers who limit their testing to their own data, avoid denial of service, and give reasonable time to remediate before disclosure.
